## 1. Who these terms are for

I am **Abdulhakeem Oladapo Raji**. These terms describe how I work with clients on communication, creative design and digital projects. You can contact me at **abdulhakeemoladaporaji@gmail.com**.

These terms apply when they are supplied or clearly identified as part of a written agreement that we both accept. Browsing the website or sending an inquiry does not, by itself, order a service or create a payment obligation.

The accepted project agreement and its scope take priority if they expressly differ from these terms. Mandatory rights under applicable law still apply. Keep the terms version supplied with your agreement; a later website update does not automatically change an existing project.

## 2. Services and scope

Services may include website development, PR/media campaigns, content and graphic design, video editing, advertising materials and consultancy, as specified in the project agreement. Future services, including possible Arabic–English document translation, are not included unless separately introduced and agreed.

Each engagement starts with a written scope describing deliverables, exclusions, price, responsibilities, timing and handover. A service name alone does not promise every possible feature or output. Paid advertising spend, domains, hosting, stock media, subscriptions, printing, ongoing maintenance and other external costs are included only if expressly listed.

Quotes remain valid for **14 calendar days**, unless the quote states otherwise. A project slot is confirmed in writing after acceptance and receipt of any agreed advance payment and required materials.

If you request additional work, I will explain its price and timing implications. I will start that extra work only after we agree to the change in writing.

## 3. Fees and payment

The project agreement and invoice identify the currency, total price, applicable taxes or charges, payment method and due dates. The default currency is **USD**, unless another currency is agreed before acceptance. There are no unannounced payment-method surcharges.

Unless the agreement specifies milestones or another schedule, project work uses **50% before work begins and 50% before final handover or a new site's public launch**. The advance payment is credited against the project fee; it is not an additional charge. Preview access may be provided for review before handover.

Invoices are due within **7 calendar days**, unless stated otherwise. An advance payment must be received before the agreed start, even where a general invoice period would run later. I may pause work after notifying you that a required payment is overdue. We will confirm any resulting schedule adjustment.

Payment can be made through **PayPal or Wise**, where available for the transaction and identified on the invoice. If PayPal is used, payment must be a commercial goods/services transaction, not a friends-and-family transfer. Processor fees charged to me are not automatically added to your bill. Your bank or payment provider may separately charge you conversion or transfer fees; any such provider terms remain separate from my invoice.

Applicable taxes and any legally required deductions must be addressed before acceptance and reflected on the invoice as appropriate. Nothing here shifts a tax obligation contrary to law or automatically requires an undisclosed gross-up.

## 4. Revisions, changes and corrections

Unless the agreement states otherwise, a project includes **two rounds of revisions** within the approved scope. One round means one consolidated set of feedback on the relevant deliverables, submitted by the agreed feedback date.

A new feature, additional page, extra format, new campaign direction or replacement brief may be a scope change rather than a revision. I will quote additional work before starting it. If no extra-work rate has been agreed, neither party should assume one.

Fixing a failure to meet the agreed specification is different from a discretionary revision. I will review reported defects and provide an appropriate correction or other remedy, subject to the agreement and applicable rights; the included revision count does not remove those rights.

## 5. Client responsibilities and approvals

Please provide accurate content, lawful materials, necessary access and consolidated feedback by the agreed dates. You are responsible for having permission to supply and use your materials. I may ask for clarification or decline materials that cannot lawfully be used.

I will ask for written approvals at the stages identified in the agreement. Silence is not automatic final acceptance. If an essential response is missing, I will send a reminder. After **30 calendar days** without the required response, I may give written notice that work is paused. Restart timing depends on availability and any agreed changes; a pause does not automatically forfeit all money already paid.

Do not send passwords or sensitive records through the public contact form. We will agree an appropriate access or file-sharing method where the work requires it.

## 6. Timing, delivery and support

The agreement states whether delivery dates are estimates or firm milestones and identifies dependencies. If missing materials, feedback, scope changes or other circumstances affect a date, I will explain the issue and propose an updated plan. Neither party should assume a missed date is automatically excused in every circumstance.

The agreement should define the review period, acceptance criteria, handover format, source files included and any post-delivery support. Hosting renewals, subscriptions, ongoing maintenance, new features and future platform changes are separate unless included in writing.

I aim to reply to inquiries within **one business day**. This is a response target, not a promise that the requested work will be completed within that time. Working days, time zone and project-specific response expectations should be stated in the agreement.

Creative work, websites and campaign materials cannot by themselves guarantee sales, search rankings, ad approval, audience growth or another business result unless a specific measurable commitment is expressly agreed.

## 7. Ownership and third-party material

After full payment of the agreed fee, the rights to original final work created specifically for the project transfer as stated in the project agreement and to the extent those rights can be transferred. The agreement must identify which source/editable files are included.

Your existing materials remain yours. My pre-existing tools, reusable code, methods, templates and rejected concepts do not automatically transfer. Where any of my retained material is incorporated in an agreed final deliverable, the agreement must give you the rights needed to use that deliverable for its intended purpose.

Third-party fonts, stock assets, software, plugins and other materials remain subject to their own licenses, costs and restrictions. I will identify relevant dependencies in the scope or handover. I cannot transfer ownership I do not hold.

I may withhold unpaid final handover where permitted by the agreement and law. This does not authorize deleting your data, taking over your accounts, or removing a previously delivered live site without a separate lawful basis.

## 8. Portfolio permission

Portfolio or social-media display requires your explicit written permission. The agreement records whether you allow display, what may be shown and when. If permission is not given, I will not display the project as a portfolio example. Confidential information and personal data are not automatically cleared for publication by general portfolio permission.

## 9. Cancellation, settlement and refunds

Either party should request cancellation in writing. We will identify the work completed, any approved non-recoverable external costs, the payments already received and the material available for handover.

If you cancel before work starts, advance payments are refundable, except for separately approved, documented and lawfully recoverable costs already incurred. A reserved start date alone is not an automatic reason to retain the entire advance payment.

If you cancel after work starts, charges for completed work are calculated using the cancellation valuation stated in the agreement: completed milestones, an agreed allocation, or an agreed time-based rate. No unagreed hourly rate is imposed after cancellation. Payments already received are credited once against those charges. Any excess is refunded; any outstanding amount must be explained in an itemized settlement. Recoverable charges remain subject to applicable law and remedies for defective work.

If I cannot continue, I will notify you, refund amounts attributable to undelivered work and arrange an appropriate handover of usable completed work covered by payment. Mandatory rights and remedies remain available; cancellation by me is not an automatic justification to charge for unusable work.

Refund amounts, method and expected processing dates will be confirmed in writing. Refunds normally use the original payment route where supported. My internal refund processing and a bank/provider's settlement time are different. A provider retaining its fee does not automatically permit me to deduct it from a refund owed to you.

Please contact me promptly about a problem so I can review it. This request does not remove your right to raise a payment-provider dispute, seek a legal remedy or meet an external deadline.

## 10. Consultancy sessions

If we agree a consultancy session, its price, duration, preparation, payment and cancellation/rescheduling terms will be stated in the booking confirmation. The project 50/50 schedule does not automatically apply to a session. No session expiry, no-show charge or refund deadline applies unless clearly agreed and legally permitted.

## 11. Confidentiality and personal information

I will use non-public client information for the agreed engagement and keep it confidential, except where disclosure is authorized, necessary through an agreed provider arrangement, or required by law. The duty does not prevent use of information already lawfully public or independently known without a confidentiality restriction.

If the project requires access to customer records or other personal information beyond an initial inquiry, we must agree roles, permitted processing, tools, access, location and return/deletion arrangements before that processing begins. A general website privacy notice does not replace a necessary project-specific data agreement.

My final Privacy Policy will explain the website's inquiry handling. Its intended URL is **https://abdulhakeemor.com/privacy.html**; an approved copy must be available before this reference is used in an accepted agreement.

I will perform agreed services with reasonable care and skill. Neither this document nor an individual agreement excludes rights or liabilities that applicable law does not allow the parties to exclude.
