Provider and project agreement

The service provider is Abdulhakeem Oladapo Raji. Contact: abdulhakeemoladaporaji@gmail.com. Browsing the website or sending an inquiry does not by itself order a service or create a payment obligation.

The project agreement identifies the policy version supplied to the client. Expressly agreed project-specific provisions take priority over these general policies, subject to mandatory legal rights. Later website updates do not automatically change an accepted agreement.

Scope, quotes and changes

The written scope lists deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, fees and timing. Hosting, domains, advertising spend, subscriptions, third-party licenses, printing and ongoing maintenance are included only when listed.

Quotes are valid for 14 calendar days unless stated otherwise. Extra work requires written agreement on its price and timing before it starts. A project slot and start conditions are confirmed in writing.

Fees and payment

USD is the default currency unless another currency is agreed. The agreement and invoice identify the total price, applicable taxes and agreed charges, payment method and due dates. PayPal or Wise may be used where available for the transaction and identified on the invoice.

Unless a different milestone or session schedule is agreed, project payments are 50% before work begins and 50% before final handover or a new website's public launch. The advance payment counts toward the total fee. Invoices are due within 7 calendar days; required advance payment must still be received before work starts.

There are no undisclosed payment-method surcharges. The client's bank or payment provider may separately charge its own transfer or conversion fees. Any required taxes or deductions must be addressed according to law and reflected in the agreed billing arrangements.

Revisions and corrections

The default project allowance is two rounds of revisions within the approved scope. Each round is one consolidated set of feedback. New pages, features, formats or a new direction may require a separate quote. An extra-work rate is not assumed if none has been agreed.

A failure to meet the agreed specification is different from a discretionary revision. The revision allowance does not remove applicable rights to correction or other remedies.

Materials, timing and pauses

Clients provide accurate materials, necessary permissions and feedback by the agreed dates. Delivery dates depend on the written scope and its dependencies. I will explain material delays and propose an updated plan rather than silently changing deadlines.

After a reminder, I may give written notice that a project is paused if a required client response has been missing for 30 calendar days. An overdue required payment may also lead to a notified pause. Restart timing and any scope change must be confirmed; a pause does not automatically forfeit every payment.

I aim to reply to inquiries within one business day. Project working days, review periods, delivery criteria and support are set in the agreement. Business results such as sales, rankings or campaign reach are not guaranteed merely by purchasing a service.

Ownership, confidentiality and portfolio use

The agreement states what rights in original final work transfer after full payment and which source files are included. Client-supplied materials remain the client's. Background tools and third-party material do not automatically transfer ownership; necessary licenses and restrictions must be identified.

Non-public project information is used for the agreed engagement, with confidentiality and any necessary provider/access arrangements set out in the agreement. Project records involving personal data may require additional handling terms.

The agreement records whether the client permits portfolio or social-media display, what may be shown and when. Confidential information, personal data and credentials are not automatically cleared for publication by general portfolio permission.

Cancellation, refunds and disputes

The project agreement must explain cancellation, valuation of completed work, approved external commitments and refund arrangements before acceptance. Payments already received must be accounted for when calculating any settlement. No blanket no-refund rule is adopted here.

Please contact me promptly about concerns so we can try to resolve them directly. This does not remove payment-provider dispute rights or applicable legal remedies and deadlines.

These general website policies do not select a particular country's law, an exclusive court or mandatory arbitration. Applicable law and mandatory rights continue to apply. Any project-specific choice of law or dispute process must be expressly agreed and appropriate to that engagement.
