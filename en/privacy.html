Who I am and how to contact me

I am Abdulhakeem Oladapo Raji, based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. I operate this portfolio and services website and am responsible for the personal information I collect through it.

For privacy questions or requests, email abdulhakeemoladaporaji@gmail.com.

Information you provide

The contact form collects your name, email address, subject and message. You may also provide a service preference and preferred timeline. Direct emails may include information and attachments you choose to send.

Please send only what is relevant to your inquiry. Do not use the public form to send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or confidential records belonging to someone else. Without a contact address and enough detail, I may be unable to respond.

Technical information and preferences

Namecheap hosting records server access information, which can include IP addresses, requested pages or files, timestamps, browser information, referring pages and response status. Hosting-provided statistics may be derived from these logs.

The contact form's anti-spam limiter stores a hashed IP identifier, attempt counter and expiry time, not the message text. It counts attempts within a one-hour window. Expired records are removed when a later submission processes the file; deletion is not guaranteed exactly one hour after an attempt.

Your language and appearance selections can be saved in local storage in your browser until cleared or removed by the browser. The website does not set advertising or analytics cookies and has no separate client-side analytics service or newsletter. Article discussions use the functional cookie described below.

How I use information

I use inquiry information to reply, assess a request and discuss possible work. Technical information helps deliver and secure the website, limit spam and diagnose problems. Browser preferences remember your choices on that device.

I do not sell your personal information or use the website to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Automatic spam checks may limit a form submission; you can contact me directly by email instead.

Access and service providers

I am the only person within my business who manages inquiries. Namecheap provides website hosting and mail transport. The form sends inquiries to my Google Gmail inbox at abdulhakeemoladaporaji@gmail.com. These providers process information as needed to provide their services.

I operate from Saudi Arabia; hosting and email services may process information in other countries. Their processing and retention depend on the services and applicable arrangements. Contact me for information about the providers used for a particular inquiry or project.

Fonts, icons and portfolio media are served from this website's hosting. Following a social link or using a sharing application takes you to a separate service, whose privacy practices apply when you use it.

Retention and deletion

I keep inquiry correspondence for up to 12 months after the inquiry closes, for recordkeeping, and then delete it. This is a mailbox-management commitment, not an automatic function of the contact form.

Records needed for an agreed project or a legal obligation may require a separate retention schedule. Hosting logs, email-provider systems and backups have their own retention arrangements; deleting a message from an inbox does not necessarily erase all provider-held copies immediately.

Your requests and choices

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to information about processing, access or a copy, correction, deletion and other available protections. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawal of that consent may be available. Applicable exceptions and recordkeeping obligations may affect a request.

Email your request to abdulhakeemoladaporaji@gmail.com, preferably from the address used for your inquiry. I may need information to verify the request. Do not send identity documents unless a necessary verification process has been explained. Where applicable, you may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

Comments and reactions

Article comments use a display name and comment text; no email address is required. Comments are stored on Namecheap hosting and reviewed before publication. An approved name, comment and date become public. Do not include private information or material you are not entitled to publish.

Opening the comment panel or liking an article creates a functional cookie for up to 30 days. Its random browser identifier helps prevent duplicate reactions, protect submissions and recognize your own comments. It is not an advertising identifier. Clearing the cookie may remove your ability to manage an existing comment from that browser.

Approved comments remain until removed. You can delete your own comment while the original browser identifier is available, or request removal by email. Pending comments older than 30 days are removed when discussion cleanup next runs. Anonymous aggregate like totals may remain after browser-identifying reaction records expire.

Abuse prevention uses keyed hashes of IP addresses with attempt counters and one-hour expiry times. Expired limits and reaction records are removed on a later write/cleanup request. These pseudonymous records and private pending comments are kept outside the public website folder; ordinary hosting logs are separate.

Security and changes

The website is configured for HTTPS, and the form validates fields and limits repeated submissions. Two-factor authentication is enabled on my Namecheap account. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

I will update this notice when the website or its information handling changes and provide additional notice or choices where required.
