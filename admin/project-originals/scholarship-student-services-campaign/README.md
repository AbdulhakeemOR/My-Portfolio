# Scholarship Student Services Awareness Campaign

Original artwork and videos supplied for the January–June 2024 graduation project. The campaign was approved by the Scholarship Office at King Abdulaziz University and was not implemented.

- `background.png` and `poster-1.png` through `poster-6.png` preserve the supplied PNGs.
- `pattern-1.mp4` and `pattern-2.mp4` preserve the supplied videos.
- `background-en-copy.png` preserves the identical background previously placed in `en/`.

Public web copies are in `assets/images/scholarship-student-services-campaign/` and `assets/videos/scholarship-student-services-campaign/`. The images are resized WebP exports; the two public videos preserve the original streams. The gallery also has preview frames for both videos. This archive is excluded from `dist/` by the public-site build.

Project text, media order, captions, and homepage inclusion are maintained in `content/projects.json`. Arabic translations are in `content/i18n/ar.json`.
