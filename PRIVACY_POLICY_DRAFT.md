# Privacy Policy

abdulhakeemor.com · Last updated: **[FINAL REVISION DATE]**

## 1. Who I am

I am Abdulhakeem Oladapo Raji, based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. I operate abdulhakeemor.com as a portfolio and a way to discuss my communication, creative design and digital services.

I am responsible for the personal information I collect through this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact me at **contact@abdulhakeemor.com**.

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website or contact me, how it is used, and how you can make a request about your information.

It covers the website and initial inquiries. If a project requires additional information, documents or tools, I will explain any relevant additional handling before you provide those materials.

## 3. Information collected

### Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, subject and message. You may also provide a service preference and preferred timeline. If you email me directly, I receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send.

Please include only information relevant to your inquiry. Do not send passwords, identity documents, payment-card details or confidential third-party records through the contact form.

Providing information is your choice. Without an email address and enough information about your inquiry, I may be unable to respond or assess your request.

### Technical information

The hosting service records technical information in server access logs. This can include your IP address, the requested page or file, the time of the request, browser/device information in the user-agent header, referring page information where provided, and response status.

The contact form also uses an anti-spam limit. It records a hashed version of the connection's IP address, an attempt counter and an expiry timestamp. The limiter does not store the message body. A hashed IP identifier is not the same as anonymous information.

### Preferences on your device

The website can store your selected language and light/dark appearance in your browser's local storage. These preferences remain on that browser until you clear them or the browser removes them.

## 4. How I use information

I use the information described above to:

- Reply to inquiries and discuss possible projects or services.
- Maintain the correspondence needed to handle a request.
- Deliver and secure the website, limit spam and investigate technical problems.
- Remember your language and appearance preferences on your device.

I do not sell your personal information. I do not use this website to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Automatic spam checks may reject or limit a form submission; the direct email address remains an alternative contact route.

Do not copy an EU/UK legitimate-interest or consent claim into the final policy without establishing that it applies. If relying on consent, explain how it can be withdrawn; if relying on another basis, describe that basis accurately.

## 5. Hosting, email and other providers

The corrected website uses:

- **Namecheap:** website hosting, server/mail transport, and domain registration. The contact form runs on the hosting server and uses its mail transport to submit inquiries.
- **Namecheap business mailbox:** the destination mailbox where I receive and manage inquiries at contact@abdulhakeemor.com.

The contact form does not currently use Formspree, Netlify Forms or another external form-delivery platform. Website fonts, icons, images and portfolio videos are served from the website's own hosting rather than loaded as third-party social/video embeds.

The corrected version offers self-hosted article likes and moderated comments. It does not currently offer a newsletter or a separate client-side analytics integration. Hosting logs and any hosting-provided statistics remain separate from that statement.

Namecheap and Google process information as needed to provide their respective services. Their own policies also explain how they handle information:

- [Namecheap Privacy Policy](https://www.namecheap.com/legal/general/privacy-policy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

I am the only person within my business who accesses and manages inquiries. The hosting and email providers still process information as needed to provide their services.

## 6. Local storage, cookies and external links

The website code uses local storage for language and appearance preferences. It does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies. Clearing site data in your browser removes stored preferences and may reset your selections.

Links to social profiles take you to external services when you follow them. The Share control may open a service or application you select, or copy a link for you to share. Those services apply their own privacy practices when you use them.

If I later introduce analytics, subscriptions or third-party embeds, I will review the resulting data collection, update this notice and implement any required choices before enabling them.

### Article conversations

Commenting asks for a display name and comment, not an email address. Approved comments, their display names and dates are public. Pending comments are visible only to their author in the original browser and to me as moderator. I can approve or delete comments.

Participating in likes or comments sets a functional cookie for up to 30 days. It recognizes the browser for likes, pending comments and deletion of its own comments. Likes are browser counts, not verified people. Clearing or losing the cookie removes access to these browser-specific controls; removal can still be requested by email.

Approved comments remain until removed. Pending comments older than 30 days are deleted when cleanup next runs on a submission or moderation visit. Rate limits use hashed IP identifiers with one-hour expiry, cleared on subsequent writes. Discussion records are stored outside the public website folder on the hosting account. No comment notification emails are currently sent; I must check the moderation dashboard.

## 8. Retention and deletion

I keep inquiry correspondence for up to **12 months after the inquiry closes**, for recordkeeping, and then delete it. Records needed for an agreed project may have a separate retention schedule, which should be explained where relevant.

**IMPLEMENT BEFORE PUBLICATION — deletion routine:** Establish a routine for reviewing closed inquiries and deleting their messages and attachments when the 12-month period ends. The contact handler does not automatically delete mailbox messages. Confirm any separate project-record requirements before making a broader deletion promise.

**VERIFY — hosting logs, email and backups:** Confirm the relevant retention settings or provider practices. Deleting an email from an inbox is not necessarily immediate deletion from Trash, synced devices or provider backups. The final notice should accurately distinguish your own actions from provider-controlled retention.

The contact form's current anti-spam counter uses a one-hour counting window. Expired entries are removed when a later form submission processes the limiter file; the code does not guarantee physical deletion exactly one hour after an attempt. This window does not determine how long email correspondence or hosting logs are kept.

Browser preferences remain on your device until cleared or removed by your browser.

## 9. Requests about your information

Depending on the law that applies to your information, you may be entitled to information about its processing, access or a copy, correction, deletion, or other available rights. Where processing relies on consent, you may be able to withdraw that consent.

Send requests to **contact@abdulhakeemor.com**. Explain what you are requesting and use the email address associated with the inquiry where possible. I may need enough information to verify the request and avoid disclosing information to the wrong person. Do not send identity documents unless a necessary verification process has been explained.

Some requests may be subject to applicable exceptions or recordkeeping requirements. Where a right to complain applies, you may contact the relevant data-protection authority, including the Saudi authority where applicable.

**VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION:** Check the rights, response requirements and complaint route applicable to the final operating arrangement. This draft does not establish compliance with every visitor's jurisdiction.

## 10. Security

The website is configured to use HTTPS. The contact handler validates form fields and includes measures to limit automated submissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Two-factor authentication is enabled on my Namecheap account.

**VERIFY — account protection:** Google Account 2-Step Verification is not yet confirmed. Do not claim that it is enabled on Gmail or that separate cPanel/Webmail authentication is configured without checking those settings.

## 11. Children

This website presents my work and business services. Please do not submit personal information about a child through the general contact form.

If you believe a child's information has been submitted inappropriately, contact me so I can review the situation and take the appropriate action.


## 12. Changes and contact

I may update this policy as the website or its data handling changes. The date at the top will show when it was last revised. Where applicable, I will provide any additional notice or choice required for a material change.

For questions or requests, email **contact@abdulhakeemor.com**.
